# Appearance

This page is for anyone who wants to change how Tack looks or reads. After it you will know where to switch between light and dark, how to pick a palette, and how to change text size, zoom, typeface and motion.

Tack has a **theme** (light, dark, or follow your system), a **palette** (the accent and surface colour family) and a few reading settings. They are saved in your browser, so they follow the browser, not your account.

**Where to find them.** The sun/moon button in the sidebar footer flips between light and dark in one click. Everything else is in **Settings → Display & accessibility**; the footer's **Display & accessibility** link opens it. **Reset to defaults** on that card returns every choice to its starting value.

---

## Theme: light, dark or system

**Theme** offers **Light**, **Dark** and **System**. System follows your operating system's light or dark setting and is the default. The footer button switches to the opposite of what is showing and pins that choice from then on; pick **System** again in Settings to follow your operating system once more.

## Palette

Four palettes ship, each available in light and dark:

| Palette | Accent | Feel |
|---------|--------|------|
| **Harbor** (default) | harbor blue, with coral | soft, nautical, the default brand |
| **Teal** | teal | calm |
| **Clay** | warm terracotta | warm, earthy |
| **Graphite** | lime on neutral grey | high-contrast, understated |

Choose one under **Palette**. Every surface, accent, badge and chart changes at once, with no reload and no per-view setting.

## Text size, zoom, typeface and motion

- **Text size** scales every piece of text, on top of the size your browser or system already sets. The steps run from 87.5% to 200%.
- **Zoom** scales the whole interface, from 80% to 200%. It applies to the desktop app. In a browser tab, use the browser's own zoom: Ctrl + and Ctrl − (⌘ on a Mac), and Ctrl 0 to reset.
- **High-legibility font** switches all text to Atkinson Hyperlegible, drawn so similar letters and digits (I l 1, O 0) stay distinct.
- **Reduce motion** turns off slide, fade and pulse animations. If your system already asks for reduced motion, animations are off either way.

---

## Where your choices are kept

Appearance is **per browser**. It is not stored on the server and is not shared between computers or between people who use the same server. Clearing the browser's site data for Tack returns every choice to its default: system theme, Harbor palette, normal size.

## Accessibility

Every palette and theme combination is tuned for readable contrast, and the settings above exist so you can go further. If you change Tack's colours yourself, see [Frontend & Design System](../developer/frontend.md) for where they are defined.
